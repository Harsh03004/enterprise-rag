NORTHWIND ANALYTICS - IT SECURITY POLICY
Policy owner: Information Security Team. Effective date: 1 February 2026.

1. PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords do not need to be
changed on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is
suspected. Password managers approved by IT are permitted and encouraged.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every company account,
including email, source code hosting and the VPN. Hardware security keys are
required for administrators; other employees may use an authenticator app.
SMS codes are not an accepted MFA method.

2. DEVICES

All company laptops use full-disk encryption. Screens must lock automatically
after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are blocked on company
laptops; approved exceptions are granted by the Information Security Team for
a maximum of 30 days.

Returned laptops are securely wiped by IT within 24 hours of being received.

3. LOST OR STOLEN DEVICES

A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within
1 hour of discovering the loss. IT will remotely lock the device and revoke its
access tokens. If the device may have held Restricted data, the Information
Security Team opens a security incident.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

Company data is classified into four levels:

- Public: approved for publication, such as marketing pages.
- Internal: general business information for employees only.
- Confidential: sensitive business data, such as contracts and financial
  forecasts. Confidential data may only be shared externally under a signed
  non-disclosure agreement.
- Restricted: the most sensitive data, such as customer personal data and
  security credentials. Restricted data must never be stored on personal
  devices and must only be processed in approved systems.

5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Security incidents are rated by severity:

- SEV-1: active compromise or exposure of Restricted data. Response begins
  within 15 minutes, at any hour.
- SEV-2: likely compromise without confirmed data exposure. Response begins
  within 4 hours.
- SEV-3: policy violation or suspicious activity with no immediate risk.
  Response begins within 2 business days.

Every employee must report a suspected incident immediately; reporting a false
alarm is never penalised.

6. ACCESS REVIEWS

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for employees
who leave the company is revoked on their final working day.
