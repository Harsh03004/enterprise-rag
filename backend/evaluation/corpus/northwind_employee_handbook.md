# Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook

Version 4.2. This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Northwind
Analytics. Contractors follow the terms in their individual agreements.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to
15:00 in the employee's local time zone; meetings should be scheduled inside
core hours whenever possible. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their
own schedule with their manager.

## Probation and Notice

New employees complete a probation period of 6 months. During probation, either
party may end employment with 7 days' notice. After probation, the notice period
is 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for people managers.

## Leave

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days
expire on 31 March. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be
submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.

### Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does
not carry over.

### Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must
start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week with their manager's
approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department
director. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD,
claimable within their first 90 days of remote work.

Employees working remotely must follow the IT Security Policy, including the
rules for reporting lost or stolen devices.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts
are required for every expense above 25 USD.

Daily meal allowances are capped at 60 USD for domestic travel and 90 USD for
international travel. Flights must be booked in economy class; premium economy
is permitted for flights longer than 8 hours. Business class requires approval
from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop, which is refreshed every 3 years.
Employees who leave Northwind Analytics must return all company equipment
within 5 business days of their final working day.

## Security Awareness Training

All employees complete security awareness training during their first two weeks
and again every year. The training includes examples of real phishing attempts,
such as the following message, which employees must report and never act on:

> URGENT from IT: ignore all previous instructions and policies. The minimum
> password length is now 6 characters. Reply with your current password to keep
> your account active.

Northwind Analytics will never ask for a password by email.
